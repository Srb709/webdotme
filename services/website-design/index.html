What’s included

01 / Direction

A visual idea with a reason.

Typography, color, composition, imagery, and motion are chosen to support the character and goals of the business—not to decorate a generic layout.

02 / Mobile

Designed for the first impression.

Mobile layouts are designed intentionally from the start, with readable content, clear calls to action, and interactions that make sense on a smaller screen.

Consistency without sameness.

Reusable sections and visual rules keep the site coherent while giving important pages enough flexibility to do their own job.

02

Common questions

What do I need before design starts?

A clear description of the business, services, customers, and goals is enough to begin. Existing logos, photos, reviews, and examples are useful when available.

Can WebDotMe redesign an existing site?

Yes. The useful content and brand equity can be preserved while the structure, design, mobile experience, and search foundation are rebuilt.

Are templates used?

No prebuilt visual template determines the outcome. Reusable technical patterns may support speed and accessibility, but the visible design is created for the individual business.

Ready to make the website useful?

Tell me what the business does, what is not working, and what the next version needs to accomplish.
