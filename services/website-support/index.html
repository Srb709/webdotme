02

Common questions

Is ongoing support required?

No. The website can launch as a completed project. Ongoing support is available when a business wants regular updates or continued improvement.

Can you support a site you did not build?

Sometimes. The existing platform and code need to be reviewed first so the scope and risks are clear.

How quickly are updates completed?

Timing depends on the size and urgency of the request. Small content corrections are different from new pages or integrations, and the expected schedule is confirmed before work begins.
