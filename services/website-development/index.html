Common questions

What platform do you use?

The platform is selected around the project’s needs. WebDotMe favors fast, maintainable builds without unnecessary software or subscriptions.

Will I own the domain and website accounts?

Yes. The domain and core business accounts remain under the client’s control. WebDotMe receives only the access needed to build and launch.

Can you connect booking or contact tools?

Yes. Existing booking, contact, social, analytics, and other business links can be integrated into the website.
