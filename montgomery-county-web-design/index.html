Common questions

What kinds of Montgomery County businesses do you work with?

WebDotMe is a fit for professional services, health and wellness businesses, home services, appointment-based businesses, and independent local brands that need a clearer, more credible website.

Do you serve both eastern and western Montgomery County?

Yes. The work is handled directly through calls, email, and shared reviews, so businesses throughout the county can use the same process without an agency handoff.

Will a new website guarantee local rankings?

No honest designer can guarantee a ranking. The site will have a strong technical and on-page foundation, but long-term visibility also depends on relevance, competition, reviews, links, useful content, and time.

Can you rebuild a site without changing the domain?

Yes. In most cases the existing domain can stay in place while the website itself is rebuilt and improved. The exact launch steps are planned before anything changes.

How long does a small-business website take?

A typical small-business project takes about two to four weeks when content and feedback arrive on schedule. More involved projects get a clear timeline before work starts.
