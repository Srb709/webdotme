What I didStrategy, design, development + launch
The challengeMake complicated choices feel personal and clear.

Insurance is complicated. The website shouldn’t be.

Washington Benefits Group helps people understand personal health and Medicare coverage. The website needed to communicate expertise without feeling clinical, overwhelming or impersonal. The experience was built around calm language, clear paths and approachable design—giving visitors enough information to understand their options while making it easy to speak directly with Christine.

02

The problems to solve

An insurance website has to explain serious decisions without adding to the stress around them. Four priorities shaped the project from the beginning.

01 / Clarity

Too much information can stop people from moving forward.

Health coverage comes with unfamiliar terms and important decisions. The site needed to organize that information without overwhelming the visitor.

02 / Trust

The relationship matters as much as the service.

Visitors needed to understand that they would receive personal guidance from Christine Washington, MSA—not be passed through an anonymous system.

03 / Audience

Different visitors arrive with different questions.

The site needed clear paths for personal health coverage, Medicare guidance and people helping family members understand their options.

04 / Mobile

Important information had to remain comfortable on a phone.

Every page, call to action and contact method was designed to remain readable and easy to use on smaller screens.

03

The design direction

Calm by design

Warm, capable and easy to trust.

Deep green, soft cream and restrained typography give the site authority without making it feel institutional. Familiar language and generous spacing keep the experience comfortable.

01 / Simplify

Give every visitor a clear place to begin.

The content is divided into focused paths so visitors can find relevant information without working through a wall of insurance terminology.

02 / Personalize

Put the advisor at the center.

Christine’s name, qualifications and approach appear throughout the website, reinforcing that visitors will work with a real person.

03 / Guide

Make the next step feel easy.

Phone, email and consultation links are placed throughout the site so visitors can reach out whenever they feel ready.

A calm digital home for an important conversation.
