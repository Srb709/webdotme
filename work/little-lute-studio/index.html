LITTLELUTESTUDIO

Embroidery + mobile spray tanning
By Megan in Philadelphia.Visit live site ↗
ClientLittle Lute Studio
What I didDesign + development
The tensionTwo services. One identity.

One brand. Two completely different reasons to show up.

Little Lute Studio combines personalized embroidery with mobile spray tanning. The site needed to make that split immediately understandable without sanding away the personality that makes the business memorable. The design leans into the contrast instead of hiding it.

02

The real homepage

01 / Separate

Make the two sides obvious.

The visitor should understand the business before they have to think about navigation. Big type, distinct imagery, and the “choose your side” concept turn the unusual service mix into the idea.

02 / Personality

Keep the weird part.

The typography and composition are intentionally editorial and a little off-center. The goal was not corporate polish. It was a site that still feels like a real person made the business.

03 / Build

Make the concept survive the phone.

The layout is treated mobile-first so the oversized type, imagery, and service split still feel designed when there is almost no horizontal room to work with.

The site doesn’t explain the personality. It behaves like it.
