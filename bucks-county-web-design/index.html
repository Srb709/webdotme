03

Common questions

Do you work with businesses in both Upper and Lower Bucks?

Yes. Projects can be handled remotely and directly, whether the business is in Lower Bucks, Central Bucks, or farther north in the county.

Can one page rank in every Bucks County town?

Usually not well. A stronger approach starts with clear service pages and adds genuinely useful location content only where the business actually works and has something specific to say.

Can you redesign an existing small-business website?

Yes. The project can keep the parts that still work, replace what is holding the business back, and organize the new site around clearer services, proof, and contact paths.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. The work can be completed through calls, email, and shared reviews while still being built around the Bucks County market and the communities the business serves.

How long does a typical project take?

Most small-business websites take about two to four weeks once the needed content and feedback are available. Larger or more complex projects get a separate schedule before work begins.

Tell me what the business does, where customers come from, and what the current site is not doing well enough.
