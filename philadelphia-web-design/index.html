03

Common questions

How much does a small-business website cost?

Pricing depends on the number of pages, content, integrations, and the amount of custom design involved. After learning what the business actually needs, WebDotMe provides a clear project scope and price before work begins.

Do you work only with Philadelphia businesses?

No. Philadelphia, Bucks County, and Montgomery County are the local focus, but WebDotMe can design and build websites for businesses anywhere in the United States.

Is local SEO included?

Every build includes the technical and on-page search foundation: indexable pages, metadata, structured content, a sitemap, Search Console setup, and local relevance. Ongoing growth still depends on useful content, reviews, links, and time.

Will the website work on phones?

Yes. Each project is designed mobile-first because a phone is often the first place a potential customer meets the business.

Who will I work with?

You work directly with Steve Brooks. There is no salesperson-to-designer-to-developer handoff.

Need the site to start pulling its weight?

Tell me what the business does, what is not working, and what the website needs to accomplish.
