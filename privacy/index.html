Privacy
Privacy / plain language

Your data.Handledwith care.

Last updated September 16, 2026

WebDotMe collects only the information needed to answer inquiries, understand site performance, and improve the experience.

01 / Inquiries

What is collected.

When you submit the project form, WebDotMe receives the details you enter, including your name, email, optional phone number, business information, requested services, budget range, and project message.

02 / Use

Why it is used.

Inquiry information is used only to review your request, contact you about the project, prevent spam, and maintain an accurate record of the conversation. It is not sold.

03 / Measurement

Basic analytics.

The site may measure page visits, referral sources, button clicks, and form conversions. This helps WebDotMe understand which pages are useful and improve the website.

04 / Providers

Services that help operate the site.

WebDotMe uses hosting and database services to run the site and securely save inquiries. After submission, you can choose to open your own email or text-message app with a prepared draft addressed to WebDotMe.

05 / Retention

How long it is kept.

Inquiry records are kept only as long as reasonably needed for project follow-up, business records, security, and legal obligations.

06 / Contact

Your questions and requests.

You can ask about, correct, or request deletion of your inquiry information by emailing webdotme.design@gmail.com.
